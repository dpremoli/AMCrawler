Scheda partenza: tre proposte

Three redesigns of the Departure card, using the same Departures in each. Everything shown comes from data the app already stores; the weekday, countdown and free seats are worked out from it.

Example date: today is 28 Oct 2026. "Parte presto" appears when a Departure starts within 21 days.

Each option shows a trip within one month and one over New Year (28 Dec to 6 Jan), where the month and year change.

What each piece means

  • Countdown ("tra 16 giorni"): days from today to the start date. Highlighted in amber, with text, when under 21 days.
  • Seat blocks (card B): one block per seat, filled for each Participant, with a small gap every five. Orange when only one person has signed up, green from two. The "6/15 iscritti" text says the same in words.
  • Code (2026-0187): the Departure code, useful when contacting the office.
  • Notes: two lines, tap to expand (unchanged from today).
