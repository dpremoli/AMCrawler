How it behaves

  • Pin: outline when off, filled blue when on. Pinned trips move into "Fissati" and leave the list below. "Fissati" follows your search and filters, and you can collapse it.
  • Bell: outline when off; filled blue with an orange number when an alert is set. The seat block for that number gets an orange outline.
  • Pin and bell are independent: you can bell a trip without pinning it.
  • "Nascondi" on a pinned or belled trip removes its pin and alert.
