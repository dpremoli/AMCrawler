AMCrawler logo: riffs on the Avventure nel Mondo mark

All three keep what makes the original recognisable: rounded arches banded in its three greens over black. Each adds an idea of its own. None copies the lettering or the rider. For your own app only, not to present as an official Avventure nel Mondo mark.

Three striped arches, but the third is only a dashed outline: a group with one place still to fill, the Coordinator. The closest to the original.
